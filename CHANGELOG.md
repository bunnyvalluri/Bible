# 📜 Vachanam Changelog

All notable changes to **Vachanam (వచనం • Vachanam • वचन)** are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added
- **Core Scripture Engine**: Canonical 66 Books across Old & New Testaments in Telugu, English (KJV), and Hindi.
- **Reading Modes**: Book Mode (with antique paper texture & drop caps), Parallel Mode (tri-column translation), Compare Mode (verse-by-verse comparison modal), and Focus Mode (full-screen ambient reader).
- **AI 6-Dimensional Theological Breakdown**: Simple Explanation, 3 Key Points, Historical Context, Spiritual Meaning, Practical Life Application, and Youth Perspective.
- **Loop Engineering AI Layer**: Integrated `@vachanam/ai-engine` with `LoopRunner`, `VerificationPipeline`, state transitions, dead-letter job queues, and automated QA audit reporting.
- **Interactive Visual Diagrams**: Timelines (Creation, Life of Christ), Flowcharts (Romans Road), Concept Maps (Armor of God), Character Maps (Prodigal Son), and Mind Maps (Beatitudes).
- **Verse Artwork Studio**: HTML5 Canvas graphics generator supporting 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9 aspect ratios, customizable typography, and PNG exports.
- **Audio Bible & Floating Player**: Global persistent player with playback speed adjustments (`0.75x` – `2.0x`) and chapter narration streaming.
- **Multilingual Full-Text Search**: Instant search with suggestions, testament filtering, and query highlighting.
- **Spiritual Reading Plans**: 30-Day Gospel Journey, 90-Day Wisdom & Psalms, 1-Year Bible, and Topical Plans with IndexedDB progress tracking.
- **Zero-Auth Offline PWA**: Client-side storage of Bookmarks, Notes, and Multi-Color Highlights in IndexedDB with complete JSON Export & Import backup.
- **DevOps & Containers**: Dockerfiles for frontend/backend, `docker-compose.yml`, and GitHub Actions CI/CD workflows.
