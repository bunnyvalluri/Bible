# 👥 Vachanam User Guide & Manual

Welcome to the **Vachanam (వచనం • Vachanam • वचन)** user guide. This manual provides detailed, step-by-step instructions for getting the most out of every spiritual study tool in the application.

---

## 🎯 Target Audiences

- 🏛️ **Pastors & Preachers**: Instant access to original chapter contexts, parallel translations, and sermon artwork.
- 🎓 **Theology Students**: Historical context, 6-dimensional breakdowns, and visual diagrams.
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Families & Daily Devotions**: Daily verse meditation, audio bible narration, and simple explanations.
- ⚡ **Youth & Teen Groups**: Relatable youth perspectives, mobile story artwork, and structured reading plans.

---

## 📖 1. Bible Reader & Reading Modes

Navigate to the **Reader** tab (`/reader`) in the top navigation bar.

### Selecting Books & Chapters
1. Use the **Book dropdown** to switch between Old Testament (39 books) and New Testament (27 books).
2. Use the **Chapter dropdown** to select any chapter (1 to total chapters).
3. Use the **Previous Chapter** and **Next Chapter** buttons at the bottom of the page to read continuously.

### Switching Reading Modes
Click the **Mode Switcher** icon in the reader toolbar:
- **📜 Book Mode**: Features antique book paper styling, authentic printed fonts, and drop caps.
- **📑 Parallel Translation Mode**: View **Telugu**, **English (KJV)**, and **Hindi** side-by-side in synchronized columns.
- **🔍 Compare Mode**: Click any verse and select *Compare Translations* to view a dedicated side-by-side breakdown.
- **🧘 Focus Mode**: Enter distraction-free full-screen reading mode.

## 💡 2. AI Theological Explanations

1. Click on any verse in the reader.
2. The **Verse Action Sheet** will appear from the bottom.
3. Click **AI Theological Breakdown**.
4. A side drawer will display the full 6-dimensional analysis:
   - *Simple Explanation*
   - *3 Key Learning Points*
   - *Historical & Cultural Context*
   - *Spiritual & Doctrinal Meaning*
   - *Practical Life Application*
   - *Youth & Teen Perspective*
5. Click **Copy All Insights** to copy the complete study note to your clipboard.
6. Click the **Regenerate** button to request fresh AI insights.

---

## 🔍 3. Multilingual Scripture Search

Navigate to the **Search** tab (`/search`).

1. Type any scripture reference (e.g., `John 3:16`, `మత్తయి 5:3`, `PSA 23`) or keyword (e.g., `love`, `సమాధానం`, `शांति`, `grace`).
2. Filter by **Language** (All, Telugu, English, Hindi), **Testament** (Old or New), or **Book**.
3. Results highlight matching words and display verse citations across languages.
4. Click any search result card to jump directly to that chapter in the Bible Reader.

---

## 📊 4. Visual Biblical Explorer & Diagrams

Navigate to the **Diagrams** tab (`/diagrams`).

1. Select a category filter:
   - **Timelines**: The 7 Days of Creation, Life of Christ, etc.
   - **Flowcharts**: The Romans Road to Salvation.
   - **Concept Maps**: The Full Armor of God (Ephesians 6).
   - **Relationship Maps**: Parable of the Prodigal Son.
   - **Mind Maps**: The Beatitudes (Matthew 5).
2. Click on individual nodes or links in the diagram to inspect theological details.

---

## 🎨 5. Verse Artwork Creator Studio

Navigate to the **Verse Studio** tab (`/artwork`) or click **Create Verse Art** on any verse in the reader.

1. **Customize Scripture Text**: Edit or refine the scripture text overlay.
2. **Select Aspect Ratio**:
   - `1:1 Square`: Optimized for Instagram posts and profile pictures.
   - `9:16 Story`: Optimized for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Stories, and TikTok/Reels.
   - `16:9 Banner`: Optimized for YouTube thumbnails and church presentation screens.
3. **Choose Fine Art Background**: Select from curated biblical oil painting backgrounds.
4. **Adjust Overlay Darkness**: Fine-tune background brightness for maximum readability.
5. Click **Download High-Res PNG** to save the artwork directly to your device.

---

## 🎧 6. Audio Bible & Floating Player

Navigate to the **Audio** tab (`/audio`) or click the **Listen** icon on any chapter or verse.

1. Select your preferred audio language (**Telugu**, **English**, or **Hindi**).
2. Click **Play** on any chapter card.
3. The **Global Floating Audio Bar** will appear at the bottom:
   - **Play / Pause** toggle.
   - **Playback Speed Selector**: Click to cycle through `0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, and `2.0x`.
   - **Timeline Scrubber**: Drag slider to jump to any section of the chapter.

---

## 📅 7. Spiritual Reading Plans

Navigate to the **Reading Plans** tab (`/plans`).

1. Choose a plan:
   - **30-Day Gospel Journey**
   - **90-Day Wisdom & Psalms**
   - **One-Year Complete Bible**
   - **Peace in Times of Anxiety** (7-Day)
   - **Youth & Faith in Action** (14-Day)
2. Click the circle icon next to any day to mark it completed. Your progress is saved automatically.
3. Click the **Read** button on any day to open the assigned passages directly in the reader.

---

## 💾 8. Bookmarks, Notes & Highlights (Offline Ready)

Navigate to the **Saved** tab (`/saved`).

### Bookmarks & Highlights
- Click any verse in the reader -> click the **Bookmark** icon or pick a highlight color (**Spiritual Gold**, **Grace Green**, **Peace Blue**, **Love Red**, **Wisdom Purple**).

### Markdown Study Notes
- Click any verse in the reader -> click **Note** -> write your reflections in Markdown -> click **Save Note**.

### Backup & Multi-Device Sync
- Click **Export Data** to download all your bookmarks, notes, and highlights as a JSON file.
- Click **Import Data** on another device to restore all saved notes without requiring login.

---

## 📱 9. Installing as a Progressive Web App (PWA)

### Chrome / Edge (Desktop & Android)
1. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) (or your domain).
2. Click the **Install Vachanam** icon in the address bar.
3. Vachanam installs as a standalone native desktop or mobile app with offline capabilities.

### Safari (iOS / iPadOS)
1. Open the website in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button -> Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
